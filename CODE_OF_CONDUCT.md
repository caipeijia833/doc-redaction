# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

Project spaces must remain professional, technically focused, and safe for contributors.

Participants must not harass, threaten, discriminate against, impersonate, or expose private information about others. Do not post real legal files, personal information, credentials, restore material, exploit details for an unpatched vulnerability, or content that violates third-party rights.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, users, or the project. Security-sensitive reports must follow `SECURITY.md` rather than a public Issue.

Report conduct concerns privately to the primary maintainer after the public repository identifies that maintainer. Until then, do not include private details in a public channel.
